Your prompt is ready 🎉

Copy it, then paste it into ChatGPT or Claude. You can edit the text below first if you like.

  1. Open chatgpt.com and log in. A standard account works.
  2. Start a new chat. If you have your child's school spelling list or a worksheet they found hard, attach a photo with the + button.
  3. Paste your prompt and press Enter. Answer its questions.
  4. Check the plan, then ask for the "week 1 printable pack" and tap the download link it gives you.
  1. Open claude.ai and log in.
  2. Start a new chat. If you have your child's school spelling list or a worksheet they found hard, attach a photo with the + button.
  3. Paste your prompt and press Enter. Answer its questions.
  4. Check the plan, then ask for the "week 1 printable pack". If it builds a page, open it and choose Print → Save as PDF.
💡 Too much? Just reply: "Make it lighter", "Swap Science to Tuesdays", "Easier worksheets". A plan that gets done beats a perfect one dropped by day 4.
✏️ AI makes mistakes. Glance at each worksheet and answer key before printing, and check topics against your child's school books.
