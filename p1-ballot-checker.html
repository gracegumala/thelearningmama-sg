Where this comes from. Every figure is the Ministry of Education's own published vacancies and balloting data for the stated year. Nothing here is modelled or predicted, and each verdict shows MOE's own sentence so you can check it yourself.

Distance is approximate. Bands are straight-line distance from your postal code to a school's mapped location. MOE measures from its own reference points, and that measurement is the only one that counts. Confirm yours with OneMap School Query before you register.

Last year is not this year. A school whose line fell at 1km last year can clear further out the next, and the reverse. Treat this as the strongest evidence available, not a guarantee.
