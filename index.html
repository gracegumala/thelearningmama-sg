Questions

Good to know

How much do the tools cost?

You can try the first tool right now. As we add more tools and extras, pricing may change, and we'll always be clear about what costs money before you use it.

Do I need a paid ChatGPT or Claude plan?

No. A standard ChatGPT or Claude account is all you need.

What happens to my children's details?

The Weekly Routine Planner builds your prompt in your own browser, so nothing is sent to us. We'll say how each new tool handles your data before you use it. We recommend using first names or nicknames only.

Is this only for Indonesian families?

No. Any parent in Singapore, or planning a move here, is welcome. We simply know the Indonesian parent perspective well, and Bahasa Indonesia versions are on our wishlist.

Can I trust what the AI says?

AI can make mistakes. Use our plans as a starting point and always check school or provider details, prices and dates yourself.
